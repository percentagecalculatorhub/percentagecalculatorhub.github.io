Frequently asked questions

How do I find X percent of Y?

Multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 25 percent of 200 is 50.

How do I find a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.

What are percentage points?

The simple difference between two percentages. A rise from 5 percent to 7 percent is 2 percentage points.

How do I reverse a percentage?

Divide the final value by one plus or minus the rate as a decimal, depending on whether it rose or fell.

Why do two 10 percent rises not make 20 percent?

Because the second rise applies to the larger amount. Two 10 percent rises give 21 percent in total.

How do I convert a fraction to a percentage?

Divide the top by the bottom and multiply by 100.
